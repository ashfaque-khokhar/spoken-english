Basic Grammar

3 time zones: All tenses are just about time — Present → now; Past → before; Future → later.

4 types (real key): Each time (present / past / future) has 4 forms — Simple (normal action, just happens) · Continuous (ongoing, happening now) · Perfect (completed, already done) · Perfect continuous (ongoing + duration, since/for time).

Present: I eat (habit) · I am eating (now) · I have eaten (already) · I have been eating for 1 hour (started before, still)

Past: I ate (completed) · I was eating (ongoing then) · I had eaten (before another past) · I had been eating for 1 hour (duration in past)

Future: I will eat (plan) · I will be eating (ongoing then) · I will have eaten (by future time) · I will have been eating for 1 hour (duration in future)

Subject + Verb + (Object)

Present: I go / She goes · Past: I went / She worked · Future: I will go

Negative: don't, doesn't, didn't · Question: Do you …? / What do you want?
