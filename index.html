  • ✅ Present tense

    1. Simple present 👉 I eat food 👉 (Daily / habit)
    2. Present continuous 👉 I am eating food 👉 (Right now)
    3. Present perfect 👉 I have eaten food 👉 (Already done)
    4. Present perfect continuous 👉 I have been eating for 1 hour 👉 (Started before, still continuing)

  • ✅ Past tense

    1. Simple past 👉 I ate food 👉 (Completed in past)
    2. Past continuous 👉 I was eating food 👉 (Ongoing in past)
    3. Past perfect 👉 I had eaten food 👉 (Done before another past event)
    4. Past perfect continuous 👉 I had been eating for 1 hour 👉 (Duration in past)

  • ✅ Future tense

    1. Simple future 👉 I will eat food 👉 (Future plan)
    2. Future continuous 👉 I will be eating food 👉 (Ongoing in future)
    3. Future perfect 👉 I will have eaten food 👉 (Done before future time)
    4. Future perfect continuous 👉 I will have been eating for 1 hour 👉 (Duration in future)
  • Simple sentence: Subject + Verb + (Object). — I eat. / She reads a book.
  • Present: I/You/We/They go. — He/She/It goes. (add -s for he/she/it)
  • Past: I went. / She worked. (often add -ed, or irregular: go→went, see→saw)
  • Future: I will go. / I am going to go.
  • Negative: I do not (don't) like. / He does not (doesn't) like. / I did not (didn't) go.
  • Question: Do you like? / Does she like? / Did you go? / What do you want?
